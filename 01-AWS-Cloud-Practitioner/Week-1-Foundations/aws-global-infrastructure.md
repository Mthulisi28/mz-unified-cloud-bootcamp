\### 🗺️ Core Technical Pillars Covered This Week

Ensure you can map, define, and articulate these three foundational components before opening your console:

1\. \*\*AWS Regions:\*\* Physically isolated, independent geographic areas containing multiple AZs, chosen based on governance rules (like POPIA compliance) and latency.

2\. \*\*Availability Zones (AZs):\*\* Discrete data centers engineered with completely redundant power, cooling, and network links to prevent regional systemic failures.

3\. \*\*Edge Locations:\*\* Locally cached global nodes serving static and dynamic content via AWS CloudFront to maximize global delivery speeds.
